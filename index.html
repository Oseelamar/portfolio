My Creative Stack

  • Figma

    My primary tool for product design, systems, flows, and prototypes.

  • Framer

    For turning polished designs into responsive, interactive websites.

  • Jitter

    My go-to for adding motion, transitions, and a little more life to static ideas.

  • Claude

    For exploring ideas, solving problems, writing, and helping me move from design into code.

  • Notion

    For research, notes, documentation, planning, and everything in between.

  • Adobe Illustrator

    For creating illustrations, custom graphics, icons, and visual assets when Figma isn’t quite enough.
